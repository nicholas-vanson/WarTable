# WarTable map changes

Preserve the Axis & Allies Global 1940 Second Edition connections. WarTable may redraw the geography inside a defined edit area. Keep the surrounding board fixed.

- Before editing, identify the affected territories and sea zones, the reference version, and the perimeter points where coasts, borders and sea-zone dividers enter or leave the edit. Pin those points. If a convincing shape needs more room, redefine the perimeter explicitly before moving its anchors.
- Define each shared edge once. Derive the shoreline, terrain mask, territory fills, ownership ink and tap targets from the same faces. Preserve land and sea adjacency, impassable terrain, canals, straits and other conditional connections. A point contact alone does not give a movement connection.
- Draw ruled maritime dividers as straight segments between their intended junctions. Preserve intentional printed corners. Shape coastlines locally and place relief along identifiable geographic features. Paint land and water in final map coordinates with the established Staff Map brush and symbol scales. Moving a junction is never a reason to warp the surrounding artwork.
- Verify the rules graph and hit areas. Inspect matched before/after views at 35–40% and 200%, centered on both sides of a wrap if applicable. Check silhouettes, ruler straightness, terrain grain, relief, selection and nearby unaffected features. A passing adjacency test alone does not establish visual acceptance.
- Keep the generator, board assets and regression checks in sync. Record the reference, edit limits and visual evidence for the change.
